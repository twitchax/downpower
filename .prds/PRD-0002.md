# Summary
The first real milestone: the NRC Power Reactor Status Report ledger, from raw pages to a deployed
fleet barcode. Every unit-day from 1999 on gets a power level, its observed reason tags, an attributed
cause with provenance, and lost output, and the site at `downpower.twitchax.com` shows it as the barcode
with the stacked area of lost output beneath it.

# Problem
The NRC's daily status reports carry a free-text reason for every reduced unit on every day, and no
one has classified them at scale. That classification is the project's whole contribution
(DESIGN.md, Intent). Live nrc.gov blocks every non-browser client, so the ledger has to be built from
legitimate sources: Wayback raw captures for the reason text and EIA's bulk file for the numbers.

# Goals
1. One idempotent pipeline (raw → parse → classify → attribute → `unit_day`) serves both the backfill
   and the daily update (Decision 7).
2. Reason text for every report day Wayback holds after the 28-day hold: complete through 2024, 79%
   of 2025.
3. Every unit-day keyed by NRC docket, with an observed layer and an attributed layer.
4. The barcode hero and the stacked area, permalinkable through URL state, deployed on Fly.
5. A `/status` page that says how much of the ledger rests on which source, and what the classifier
   didn't know.

# Technical Approach

```text
 Wayback (status pages) ─┐                               catalog/ (committed)
 EIA NUC_STATUS.zip ─────┼─▶ raw store ─▶ parse ─▶ classify ─▶ attribute ─▶ Surreal: unit, unit_day
 EIA-860 (1999–2006 cap) ┘   sha256+zstd   per era   tags.toml   precedence_v1      │
                             + manifest              units.csv   inherit_14d        ├─▶ barcode buffer (ETag)
                                                     reasons.tsv                    ├─▶ stacked area (SVG)
                                                                                    └─▶ /status
```

- **Crates.** A new `crates/downpower-ingest` holds fetch, raw store, parse, classify and attribute.
  It is pure and doesn't depend on Surreal, so its tests stay fast. `downpower-server` writes its
  output to Surreal and serves it. The wasm half links neither.
- **Catalog.** `catalog/` holds the committed, reviewed tables (`tags.toml`, `units.csv`,
  `reasons.tsv`). They are embedded with `include_str!`, so the binary carries them.
  `derived_version` is a hash of the catalog plus a parser version constant. When it changes, the
  server rebuilds `unit_day` from raw in the background and keeps serving the previous build until
  the new one is done.
- **Capture selection** (Decision 10). For each report date, list the CDX captures and take the
  newest 200 capture made 28 or more days after the date. A body that fails the structural check
  (four region tables, a closing tag) falls back to the next-newest capture. Days with no usable
  capture fall to EIA numbers only.
- **Sources per unit-day.** `source` is `nrc_page`, `eia`, or `nrc_page+eia`. The NRC page wins on
  power. The disagreement rate is a `/status` metric, mostly a crosswalk check.
- **Observed and attributed** (Decision 20). Observed holds the raw string, its tags, their kinds
  and fine causes, and its flags. Attributed holds the primary cause, its family, `method`
  (`precedence_v1`, `inherit_14d`, `xref_unresolved`) and `evidence` (the tags, or the unit-day an
  inherited cause came from).
- **The barcode buffer.** One byte per unit-day, ordered by docket then date: the high 3 bits hold
  the family, and the low 5 bits hold the power bucket, with one value reserved for "no data".
  It's served as `application/octet-stream` with an ETag derived from `derived_version` and the
  last ingested date.
- **Memory.** SurrealKV's memtable and block cache are capped in `db::open` through
  `surrealdb::opt::Config::datastore_option`, sized from peak RSS measured during the first real
  backfill on the home box.

# Assumptions
- Wayback keeps serving the captures that the 2026-11-12 CDX listing counted.
- EIA's percent outage is 100 minus NRC power. This was checked for 3 units in 2024; T-007 checks it
  for every unit and year.
- Where the HTML markup differs between the 1999, 2008, 2016, 2025 and 2026 eras, the column set
  stays the same (`Unit | Power | Down | Reason or Comment | Change in report | Number of Scrams`).

# Constraints
- No request to live nrc.gov from code until the NRC grants access. No spoofed User-Agent, ever.
- Stable toolchain 1.99 (`rust-toolchain.toml`). No chart or component library (Decision 23,
  CLAUDE.md).
- A 1 GB Fly VM: the rebuild has to fit under the SurrealKV caps.
- Outward steps (`fly apps create`, `fly volumes create`, DNS) are Aaron's to run.
