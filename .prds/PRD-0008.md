# Summary
Findings: short notes, each arguing one thing a figure shows, with the figure live beside it. They
are the first prose on the site that says what the charts mean, and the step before announcing it.

# Problem
The site has four tested chart views (the home barcode and stacked area, `/trends`, `/scrams`,
`/ffd`) and the explorer. None of them says what it shows. A reader has to find the 1999-to-2016
drop in lost output, or that turbine and generator scrams cost the most energy, on their own, and
the caveats that make those readings honest sit in `docs/BREAKS.md` and the page footers. Decision
25 planned findings as markdown files with their view's state; nothing has built them.

# Goals
1. A finding format checked in CI: a note whose view, state and figure still exist, and whose query
   means today what it meant when it was written.
2. A page per finding with its figure live at the pinned state, a `/findings` index, and on each
   view a line listing the findings drawn on it.
3. Link previews from the server's HTML, so a shared finding shows its title and summary.
4. Writing one is one command from a view URL.
5. The first four findings, one per chart view. The candidates, each to be measured again:
   - **Home:** lost output fell from 14.7% of net capacity in 1999 to 8–9% since 2016, with
     refueling 5–10 points of it every year (PRD-0006.log.md#t-004).
   - **/trends:** Event Notification scrams fell from about 105 per 100 operating unit-years in
     1999 to about 40 from 2017, automatic and manual alike, and 2026 is running at about half of
     2025 (PRD-0006.log.md#t-005).
   - **/scrams:** turbine and generator scrams cost the most energy (78 of 240 TWh), offsite power
     scrams stay down longest (a median of 6 days against 4), and the 16 largest episodes carry 22%
     of the energy, San Onofre 3's 2012 episode alone 12.8 TWh (PRD-0007.log.md#t-003).
   - **/ffd:** drug-related events ran 2.9 to 11.7 per 100 operating unit-years with no visible
     trend while the adult-use share of unit-years rose from 0.1% to 43.8%, and the series is a
     floor, since reports stopped naming the substance (PRD-0005.log.md#t-007).

# Technical Approach
**Format.** `findings/NNNN-slug.md`, TOML frontmatter between `+++` lines (the catalogs are TOML,
and `toml` is already a dependency), then the note:

```markdown
+++
title = "Scrams fell by more than half, and stayed down"
summary = "One sentence for the index and the link preview."
view = "/trends"
query = "?from=1999-01-01"
figure = "scram-rate"
written = 2026-11-25
through = 2026-11-24
+++

The note, in markdown.
```

**Compiled in.** `downpower_core::findings` parses the frontmatter and renders the note with
pulldown-cmark, rejecting raw HTML, behind a `render` feature. The app's `build.rs` uses it as a
build dependency over `../../findings`, failing the build on a malformed file, and writes a static
table of findings with their HTML into `OUT_DIR`. The server and the wasm share the table, so the
server renders each finding and its tags with no fetch and the wasm carries no markdown parser. A
unit test in the app runs every finding's query through its view's state (`ViewState`) and back,
and looks its figure up on the view.

**Figures.** Each chart view draws its figures from a list of `{ id, label, meta, legend, chart }`,
so a page and a finding draw the same figure from the same code. A finding's page builds the
view's data loads and one figure at the pinned state, with no filters, and a link to
`{view}{query}#{figure}`.

**Pages.** `/findings` lists every finding, newest first, with its summary and view; a finding's
page sets `<title>`, `description` and `og:*` from its frontmatter, and shows "written {written},
on data through {through}" above the figure. Each view lists its own findings under its intro. The
nav gains `findings` after `downpower`.

**Authoring.** `cargo xtask finding <url> <slug>` takes a view URL (as copied from the browser),
canonicalizes its query, numbers the file after the highest one, and fills `written` and `through`
from today's local build. The note is written by hand.

# Assumptions
- Every chart view's state is `ViewState`, whose `to_query` writes a canonical query, so a
  round-trip is a fair test of a finding's state.
- Link previews take a title and a description; an image is not needed for the first findings.

# Constraints
- No new source and no change to the ledger: findings read the views as they are.
- pulldown-cmark is the one new dependency, and only at build time.
- One axis per chart and the existing palettes (dataviz skill); no component library.
- Stable toolchain 1.99.

# Non-Goals (MVP)
- The causal FFD analysis (Decision 4) and the heat analysis.
- Findings on `/events` or an event page.
- A chart image for link previews, an Atom feed, comments or search.
- Notes whose numbers update themselves: a note's numbers hold as of its `through` date.
- A dev-only save button (Decision 46).
