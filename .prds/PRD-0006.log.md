## T-004 -- 2026-11-18

Looked at on the home-box archive (1,016,840 unit-days through 2026-11-13, rebuilt in 86 s under
LEDGER_VERSION 10), at 1,360 px and 390 px.

- **Loss share.** 14.7% of net capacity in 1999, 10–12% through 2012, and 8–9% since 2016. Refueling
  is 5–10 points of it every year; Forced peaks at 3.0 in 2012.
- **Month of year.** Refueling dominates April (about 17%) and October (about 14%), the two refueling
  seasons. Environmental is under 0.2% in every year and invisible beside them; `families=environmental`
  isolates it.
- **Refueling outages.** 1,656 across the archive. The median falls from 36 days (1999) to 29–34
  since 2016. The longest are extended shutdowns that began as refuelings: Fort Calhoun's from 2011-04-10
  (984 days) and Davis-Besse's head-degradation outage from 2002-02-16 (758). Some one- to three-day runs count too: a startup that touches 1% for a day splits
  an outage, and the medians and quartiles ride over them.
- **Scrams.** EN scrams per 100 operating unit-years fall from about 105 (1999) to about 40
  (2017 on), automatic and manual alike.
- **Fixed on the way.** The scale's labels sat on the first bar, so bar charts put the scale in a
  40 px gutter; the scale overshot (30% for a 20% peak), so it takes five steps, as the stacked area
  does.
- **Not fixed here.** At 390 px the page is wider than the viewport, as the home page already is:
  the range row doesn't wrap. That is the open mobile-padding item, fixed with PRD-0005's page.

## T-005 -- 2026-11-19

**The release.** v0.2.0 (`ef870c3`, with PRD-0005) deployed as Fly v12 at 01:41 UTC on
2026-11-20 from its CI image. `curl -fsS` finds `Fleet trends` on `/trends` (uat-005) and
`Fitness for duty` on `/ffd` (PRD-0005 uat-006), both served before the rebuild finished, while the
data routes answered 503 and the old build kept serving the home page.

**The boot rebuild took about 12.5 minutes.** The run fetched for under a minute, began
`rebuilding the ledger` at 01:42:49 and swapped the new build in by 01:55:42, when it began dropping
the old tables. v9's full rebuild with events took about 6 minutes (PRD-0003.log.md#t-011); this
one also runs the trends and FFD collectors, and the shared vCPU's burst budget differs run to run.
Fly's `/healthz` check failed three times during it (01:46:39, 01:52:57, 01:55:03), passing again
on the next check 30 s later each time. No earlier PRD log mentions a miss.

**Live, it matches the local build.** `/api/ffd` holds 913 events (804 operating, 66 construction,
18 other reactor sites, 25 facilities) and `/api/trends` 1,656 refueling outages and 1,017,404
unit-days through 2026-11-19. Every page answers 200 in about 140 ms, and the release build draws
`/trends` and `/ffd` with no console messages.

**2026 has half the scrams.** EN scrams through 2026-11-19 are 19 (9 automatic, 10 manual)
against 39 in 2025, so the partial 2026 bar reads about 23 per 100 unit-years against about 41.
The build's scram check finds 22 status-report scrams with no EN across all 27 years, too few to
hide a shortfall of about 20, so the drop is in the reports themselves rather than in the parsing.
