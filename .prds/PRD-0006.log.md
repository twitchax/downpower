- **Loss share.** 14.7% of net capacity in 1999, 10–12% through 2012, and 8–9% since 2016. Refueling
  is 5–10 points of it every year; Forced peaks at 3.0 in 2012.
- **Month of year.** Refueling dominates April (about 17%) and October (about 14%), the two refueling
  seasons. Environmental is under 0.2% in every year and invisible beside them; `families=environmental`
  isolates it.
- **Refueling outages.** 1,656 across the archive. The median falls from 36 days (1999) to 29–34
  since 2016. The longest are extended shutdowns that began as refuelings: Fort Calhoun's from 2011-04-10
  (984 days) and Davis-Besse's head-degradation outage from 2002-02-16 (758). Some one- to three-day runs count too: a startup that touches 1% for a day splits
  an outage, and the medians and quartiles ride over them.
- **Scrams.** EN scrams per 100 operating unit-years fall from about 105 (1999) to about 40
  (2017 on), automatic and manual alike.
