# PRD-0008 log

## T-005 -- 2026-11-26

**How the numbers were measured.** A scratch binary ran `downpower_core`'s own chart functions
(`trends::years`, `triggers::years`, `ffd::years`) over the API's JSON, so a finding's numbers are
the ones its chart draws. `/api/trends`, `/api/lost/attributed` and `/api/ffd` came from production
(data through 2026-11-25); `/api/scrams` came from the local build (through 2026-11-13), since
production didn't serve it before M6's deploy. 0002's numbers are all from 2025 or earlier, which
late reasons don't move. 0003's whole-period totals (240 TWh, 1,638 episodes of 1,822 scrams) run to
the local data's last day, so both findings get measured again on production after the deploy.

**What changed from the PRD's candidates.**
- **Home moved to `/trends#loss-by-year`.** The home view's stacked area is TWh per month, and the
  fleet went from about 103 operating unit-years a year to about 94, so the drop in lost output is
  only honest as a share of capacity. The home view has no finding yet.
- **The scram rate is about 46, not about 40**, from 2016 to 2025 (100 in 1999–2001). It fell for
  every named system; turbine and generator fell least (22 to 15) and is now the most common.
- **"Turbine and generator is the only cost that grew"** held against 1999–2001 (0.19% of net
  capacity to 0.29%) and failed against 1999–2008 (0.37% to 0.29%): three early years are too few
  for a cost series this lumpy. The finding says what survives both windows: scram losses fell
  from 1.08% to 0.74%, turbine and generator least, to 40% of the total.
- **The FFD "named none" share** in the PRD-0005 log (13–25% through 2011, 42–78% from 2021) was
  re-measured as pooled shares: 18% in 1999–2011 (2008 alone was 4%), 53% in 2021–2025, and 79% so
  far in 2026.
