## T-003 -- 2026-11-13

`downpower_ingest::status::parse` read every stored status page, the latest capture of each date, in
31 s (release build, decompression and hash checks included), with no errors.

- 10,145 pages carry all six columns, and 27 (2026-10-17 to 2026-11-12) are inside the 28-day hold
  with `Unit | Power` only. In all: 1,022,782 unit-days, 110 unit names, and 24,442 distinct reason
  strings.
- Seven read "No data available for this date." with no tables, under a 200. 2026-11-13 wasn't
  published yet. The other six (1999-05-08, 2013-07-18, 2013-12-21, 2015-12-02, 12-03 and 12-04)
  read the same on a second fetch a day later, so they are permanent gaps.
- Every page has exactly four region tables, in order. Power is always an integer from 0 to 100,
  `Down` is ISO or blank, `Change in Report (*)` is `*` or blank, and `Scrams (#)` is blank or `1`,
  first on 2013-06-29.
- Two pages list units twice, and the parser keeps every row, since it reports the page as written.
  - 2006-06-22 repeats five units (Vogtle 1, Vogtle 2, Watts Bar 1, Waterford 3, Wolf Creek 1). The
    copies agree except that one Wolf Creek 1 row has the change mark.
  - 2013-07-19 repeats all 100 units, each pair adjacent, and 34 pairs disagree (e.g., Hope Creek 1
    at 92% and 87%). One copy spells `LaSalle 1/2`, as the 2013-07-17 page does, and the other
    `La Salle 1/2`, as the 2013-07-20 page does. The page probably carries the missing 2013-07-18
    report as well, but nothing on it says which copy is which.
  - So the step that keys rows by docket (T-008) merges copies that agree and needs a rule for copies
    that don't. The proposal: EIA power for those unit-days, with both reasons kept as observed text.
- Pages from 2025 on put a lone NUL in a blank reason cell. html5ever drops it inside a table cell,
  as the HTML spec says it must, so the parser needs no stripping; two tests pin that.
- Fixtures: eight pages (1999-01-05, 1999-05-08, 2008-07-15, 2013-06-29, 2016-07-15, 2025-07-15,
  2026-10-15, 2026-11-12) copied byte for byte from the raw store. `.gitattributes` marks them
  `-text`, so git never rewrites their CRLFs.

## T-004 -- 2026-11-13

`catalog/units.csv` holds 107 units: every unit name on the status pages since 1999 (110 spellings),
each matched one-to-one to one of the 107 generators in `NUC_STATUS` (fetched 2026-11-13).

- **Dockets** come from the NRC's List of Power Reactor Units (95 operating, Vogtle 3 and 4 on
  `052` Part 52 dockets) and its decommissioning list (the 12 retired units).
- **Name variants.** `La Salle` and `LaSalle` alternate between 2013-06-27 and 2016-09-26; `River
  Bend 1` became `River Bend Station 1` on 2019-03-23; the NRC's own list says `Shearon Harris 1`,
  which is the display name, while the pages say `Harris 1`.
- **Shutdown dates** come from each unit's NRC facility page and are the last day in service: the
  morning report usually still shows the unit at power. For 9 of the 13, the last day above 0% on
  the pages is exactly that date. The exceptions:
  - Crystal River 3 (last at power 2009-09-25) and San Onofre 2 and 3 (2012-01) sat in forced
    outages until retirement, so their outage years are lost output and the NRC's dates stand. San
    Onofre's page gives only "certified in June and July 2013"; the date used is the 2013-06-07
    announcement, and the pages read "PERMANENT PLANT SHUTDOWN" from 2013-06-08.
  - Three Mile Island 1's pages show power through 2019-09-20 against the NRC's 2019-09-26, so six
    days at 0% count as in service.
- **Palisades** shut down 2022-05-20, and its operator rescinded the shutdown certifications on
  2025-08-25. It has shown no power since, so `restart` stays blank and the unit stays out of
  service until its first day above 0%. That is a judgment call: counting from the rescission
  instead would book about 15 months of restart work as lost output.
- The fixtures `tests/fixtures/status/unit-names.txt` and `tests/fixtures/eia/nuc_status_generators.tsv`
  freeze both lists, so CI checks the catalog against them in both directions.
