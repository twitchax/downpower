# Summary
A `/trends` page of four charts over the fleet's 27 years, each a rate so a fleet that changes
size doesn't skew it: reported power reduction as a share of net capacity by family and year, the
same by month of the year, refueling outage length by year, and Event Notification scrams per 100
operating unit-years.

# Problem
The barcode and the stacked area show absolute losses day by day. Neither answers whether the fleet
is losing less output than it did, which causes moved, or when in the year the losses fall. A
fleet that ran about 103 unit-years in 1999 and 94 in 2025 makes raw totals misleading across
years.

# Goals
1. The share of net capacity lost per year, by family, following the layer and the families shown.
2. The month-of-year profile of the same share, so the refueling seasons and the summer
   environmental losses show without weather data.
3. Refueling outage length by year: the median and the interquartile range, with the count.
4. EN scrams per 100 operating unit-years by year, automatic and manual.

# Technical Approach
- **The record.** A rebuild collects, per month, the in-service unit-days (rows), their net
  capacity energy (`capacity_kw` × 24 over rows that have one, the same rows `lost_kwh` is weighed
  on) and the EN scrams on catalog units by kind; and every refueling outage. It is stored per
  build as `trends:[table]` and served as JSON from `/api/trends`. The loss shares divide
  `/api/lost/{layer}`'s monthly kWh by the capacity energy of the same months, so the layer switch
  costs nothing new.
- **Refueling outages.** A unit's run of consecutive days at 0% power, with a day at power before
  and after it (so runs cut by the archive's edges, a shutdown or the present don't count), is an
  outage. It is a refueling outage when Refueling is its most common attributed family. Its length
  is its days at 0%, and it belongs to the year it starts in. The morning snapshot makes this an
  approximation of breaker-open to breaker-closed.
- **Scrams.** An unretracted reactor event's unit with a SCRAM Code of `A/R` or `A` is automatic,
  `M/R` or `M` manual, counted in the month the event occurred. Exposure is the month's
  in-service unit-days over 365.25.
- **The page.** `/trends` takes the home page's `ViewState`: the range picks the years (and the
  months the seasonal profile averages over), the layer and families drive the two loss charts,
  and `table=1` opens every chart's table. Bars are a new pure geometry module beside `area.rs`. The
  newest year is drawn hatched and labeled partial when the data ends before December 31.

# Assumptions
- Rows exist only for in-service days (Decision 16), so rows are the operating exposure.
- A 0% morning report is a reactor that is shut down; a startup that reaches the grid before the
  next morning shows above 0%.

# Constraints
- One axis per chart, no chart library, stable 1.99.
- No new data source; everything rides on the existing build.

# References to Code
- `crates/downpower-server/src/ledger.rs`: `Packers`, where every row of a build passes.
- `crates/downpower-core/src/series.rs`: the per-build series pattern.
- `crates/downpower-ingest/src/scrams.rs`: the SCRAM Code reading the scram counts share.
- `crates/downpower-app/src/lost.rs`, `area.rs`, `filters.rs`: the chart, geometry and filters to reuse.

# Non-Goals (MVP)
- Per-unit or per-plant trends (a unit page is a later PRD).
- Weather, cooling type or generation joins (Decision 26's sequence).
- Statistical tests of a trend; the charts are descriptive.
