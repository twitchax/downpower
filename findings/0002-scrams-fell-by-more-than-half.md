**Event Notification scrams fell from 100 per 100 operating unit-years in 1999–2001 to 47 in 2016–2025, and every system the reports name as the trigger fell.** Reactor and coolant system scrams fell the most, from 21 to 8. Plant electrical went from 17 to 5, and feedwater from 22 to 12.

Turbine and generator scrams fell the least (from 22 to 15), so they are now the most common trigger, about a third of all scrams. Scrams during testing fell from 12.6 to 3.0, and during maintenance from 6.2 to 1.2. The one rise is in scrams whose report names no system at all, from 0.6 to 2.1.[^1]

[^1]: A scram's system is the one its Event Notification says started it, read by phrase rules that agreed with a hand-labeled check on 39 of 50 events (and on the circumstance, 43 of 50). It is what tripped as filed, which is often a protective signal rather than a root cause.
